## 🎤 Final Words

### From the Instructors

**Remember**:

1. **Start Small**
   - You don't need to transform everything at once
   - One assignment is a great start
   - Iterate and improve over time

2. **Stay Connected**
   - The community is here to support you
   - No question is too small
   - Share both successes and struggles

3. **Focus on Learning**
   - AI is a tool to enhance education, not replace it
   - Student understanding is still the goal
   - Process matters as much as output

4. **Be Patient**
   - With yourself
   - With students
   - With the technology
   - It gets easier!

5. **Lead the Way**
   - Your students are watching
   - Model good AI use
   - Show continuous learning
   - Embrace the future

### You Are Future-Makers

> "The future of education isn't about avoiding AI. It's about teaching students to think critically, create thoughtfully, and collaborate effectively in a world where AI is ubiquitous. You are leading the way."

**Thank you for**:
- Your openness to new ideas
- Your willingness to experiment
- Your commitment to students
- Your contribution to education's future
